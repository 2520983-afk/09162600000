FOR MY BABY CY

Hi baby,

idk saan ako magsisimula, wala pa akong nasusulat and naiiyak na ako hahahaha. andito ako sa bahay ngayon, walang internet and signal, and ang ginagawa ko lang ngayon is isipin ka. then i started realizing kung gaano pala kalaki yung naging impact mo sa buhay ko.

thank you so much kasi nandyan ka palagi for me. i really, really appreciate you, cyril. i know i'm not always easy to deal with. minsan maldita ako, minsan ang kulit ko, minsan nag-iinarte ako for no reason, and i know nakakainis din ako sometimes. pero kahit ganon, you never make me feel like i'm too much.

you still try to understand me, and you never make me feel bad for having feelings.

i know i'm not really the sweet or affectionate type. hindi ako sanay mag-express ng feelings ko kasi ganon ako lumaki. i'm not the type of person who easily says what i feel or shows affection all the time.

pero simula nung dumating ka, slowly, natuto ako. natuto akong magsabi ng nararamdaman ko, natuto akong maging grateful, and natuto akong mas pahalagahan yung mga taong nandyan talaga for me.

and i think that's one of the things i appreciate most about you.

actually, kahit nung friends pa lang tayo, na-appreciate ko na talaga yung pagkatao mo. hindi ko naman kailangan maging close sayo para makita kung gaano ka-genuine na tao.

you're genuinely kind and respectful, and sana alam mo kung gaano ka kabuting tao.

kaya kapag nagdo-doubt ka sa sarili mo, sana maalala mo rin kung paano ka nakikita ng mga taong nagmamahal sayo.

because i see you, baby.

i see how hard you try, how you treat people, how you care, even with the little things.

and of course, i love your corny jokes, yung mga maling spelling mo, and all the little things that make you you.

honestly, minsan hindi ko alam kung matatawa ako or maiinis sayo. and yes, bonus na lang talaga na gwapo ka.

sorry din baby kung minsan ino-overthink ko yung mga bagay-bagay. sorry kung may trust issues ako and kung may times na hindi ko ma-explain nang maayos kung bakit ako ganon.

i know it can be tiring sometimes, and i really appreciate you for being patient with me. instead of making me feel like my feelings are a problem, you listen.

you try to understand where i'm coming from. you validate what i feel, and you make me feel heard.

i don't think you realize how much that means to me.

baby, you were my first in so many things. you were the first person who made me feel this kind of love.

you made me realize things about myself that i didn't really notice before. you made me want to change some of my wrong habits, and somehow, you made things feel a little easier whenever i was having a bad day.

i never thought that someone who started as just my classmate would become one of the most important people in my life.

we started as classmates, then naging friends. lumaki yung cof natin, naging mas close tayo, and then noong december, i finally confessed to you.

hanggang sa hindi ko na rin namalayan na yung friendship natin naging something more.

and honestly, i wouldn't change how it happened.

i like that we didn't force anything. everything just happened naturally. we got closer, we got to know each other more, and eventually, we became this.

then noong august 11, you started courting me.

i know hindi pa naman ganon katagal. pero if i'm being honest, ayoko na rin patagalin pa.

not because i'm trying to rush us or because i don't appreciate the effort you're putting into courting me. actually, i appreciate it so much.

but i think i just finally reached the point where i don't want you to keep waiting for an answer that i already know.

because i already know how i feel about you.

and i already know that i want you.

so baby...

sinasagot na kita.

i want to be your girlfriend.

boyfriend na kita, baby. ♡

i want us to be official, and i want it to be with you. alam ko naman na dun din papunta lahat ng to, and i don't want to keep you waiting just because i'm scared to finally say it out loud.

you already mean so much to me, and i think you deserve to know that.

and yung pinakilala mo ako sa family mo nung june pa lang, before you even officially started courting me, hindi ko nakakalimutan yun.

maybe it seemed like a small thing to you, but it meant a lot to me. it made me realize na hindi mo lang ako basta gusto.

you were serious about me, and i really appreciated that.

you make me feel safe enough to be myself. i don't feel like i have to act a certain way around you just so you'll stay.

i can be quiet, makulit, maldita, emotional, or whatever version of myself i happen to be that day, and you still make me feel accepted.

and i hope i can give you that same feeling.

i hope you feel like you can be yourself with me too.

i don't know exactly what the future looks like for us, and i don't want to make big promises about things we haven't experienced yet.

but right now, i know that i want to keep choosing you and see where this takes us.

i'm really thankful that out of all the people i could have met, ikaw yung naging classmate ko, naging friend ko, and eventually became the person i fell in love with.

thank you for being patient with me. thank you for understanding me even when i'm hard to understand.

thank you for making me feel loved without making me feel like i have to earn it.

and thank you for being you, baby.

i love you so much, cyril.

more than i probably know how to explain properly.

and ayun, i guess wala ka nang kailangan hintayin na sagot from me.

sinasagot na kita.

boyfriend na kita, baby. ♡

— baby j ♡
